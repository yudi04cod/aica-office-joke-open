# Aica Office Joke Open

One line of office comedy for the [Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun): a single joke about office life, under 200 characters.

## The joke

> HR asked why I CC'd the office plant on every email — it's the only one here that listens, waters itself, and never replies all.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic) writes the joke to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).

Run `python3 main.py` to produce the output file.
